Automation

Whatever can run by itself does: recording, departure, arrival, finish.

The limit: the app can only cast off by itself while it receives data from the onboard network. Only then is it certain that the iPhone is on board and not in the car on the way to the bakery. Without an onboard network, casting off is one tap. Automatic mooring works with GPS alone.

Read more in the handbook

  • Cast off with one tap

    Voyage and departure entry are created at once. The app adds place name and wind, and carries over the sails you used last.

    FreeiPhone GPS

  • Automatic departure

    Once the boat makes more than 1.5 kn for over a minute, the departure entry is created by itself, backdated to when you got under way. The minute filters out shifting berth in harbour. A notification confirms “Departure detected”.

    FreeOnboard network

  • Auto tracking

    Records the track in the background, with the iPhone locked and after an app restart too. One point every 30 s under way, every 5 s in a race, every 5 min when stationary; after 30 min without movement it pauses and resumes when you move.

    FreeiPhone GPS

  • Automatic mooring at the stage destination

    When the boat reaches the arrival radius of the chosen destination, “Mooring (stage)” is created and recording stops. The voyage stays open; nobody has to reopen it.

    FreeiPhone GPS

  • Mooring without a set destination

    If the boat enters the radius of a harbour from your harbour guide slowly (under 1.5 kn), a stage mooring is created as well. Sailing past at speed does not count.

    FreeiPhone GPS

  • Voyage closes at the final destination

    With a final harbour and radius set in the voyage, “Arrival” is created there and the voyage closes as soon as the boat is back after having left the radius once.

    FreeiPhone GPS

  • Standstill suggestion

    If the boat has been stationary for two hours, the app suggests closing the voyage, backdated to the moment the boat came to rest.

    FreeiPhone GPS

  • Correct departure and fill the gap

    If the stretch from the berth to the detected departure is missing, one tap moves the entry to the berth and fills the gap in the track.

    FreeiPhone GPS

  • Motoring distance and engine hours

    Casting off under engine sets “Engine on”. Leaving harbour counts as motoring, yellow on the map, and engine hours go into the statistics.

    FreeYour input

  • Weather pre-filled in the entry

    Wind and air pressure are already in the logbook entry: from the onboard network, otherwise from the nearest station or the model; air pressure from the sensor in the iPhone.

    FreeOnboard networkInternetiPhone sensor

  • Race ends at the finish line

    When the boat crosses the finish line after sailing the course, the finish entry is created, the countdown stops and a notification reports the finish.

    FreeiPhone GPS

  • Course advances by itself

    After each rounding the bar shows the next mark with side, bearing and distance.

    FreeiPhone GPS

  • Harbour guide fills itself

    Every destination you choose ends up in the harbour guide with an arrival radius.

    Free

Onboard network (NMEA)

One gateway on the boat’s Wi-Fi turns the iPhone into an instrument. Free records everything the onboard network delivers; Pro works it out later.

How to set up the onboard network

Read more in the handbook

  • NMEA 2000 over Wi-Fi

    Reads NMEA 2000 in RAW format over TCP, as sent for example by the Yacht Devices YDWG-02N. Its factory address is already filled in.

    FreeOnboard network

  • NMEA 0183 over Wi-Fi

    Reads NMEA 0183 sentences from any Wi-Fi gateway or multiplexer, over TCP or UDP.

    FreeOnboard network

  • Format is detected

    You only choose transport, address and port. Whether 2000 or 0183 arrives, the app reads from the data.

    FreeOnboard network

  • Connects by itself and keeps watch

    Connects at launch, reconnects after a drop and notices when the connection is up but nothing arrives any more.

    FreeOnboard network

  • Instruments from the onboard network

    Speed through water, true and apparent wind, depth, water temperature, air pressure with trend, log and trip on four standard pages. If the bus delivers apparent wind only, the app works out true wind.

    FreeOnboard network

  • Wind at every track point

    True wind and tack are stored along with the track. This produces the tack colouring of the track and every analysis that uses wind.

    FreeOnboard network

  • Boat by heading on the map

    Heading line from the compass on the network, plus the course-over-ground vector.

    FreeOnboard network

  • Laylines, line bias, sail suggestion

    Laylines from the mark on the map, start line bias in the pre-start, and at a sail change the sails that suit the wind listed first.

    FreeOnboard network

  • Check instruments

    One line per sensor: delivering, silent, stuck or implausible. Shows at the dock which sensor is missing.

    FreeOnboard network

  • Calibration and damping

    Masthead offset, log factor, heel offset, mirror wind angle, manual variation and damping for wind, speed and course. Damping only smooths the display; the track keeps the measured values.

    FreeOnboard network

  • Record raw data

    Records the bus as a raw log, with an archive, remaining storage time and an economy mode.

    FreeOnboard network

  • Your own instrument pages

    Put pages together yourself, swap tiles, and use smart fields that show the right value for the situation.

    ProOnboard networkiPhone GPS

  • Sailing compass

    Laylines, tack sectors and target angles from the polar on one page.

    ProOnboard networkPolar

  • Regatta page in the dashboard

    VMG against the polar, tacking angle, distance to the layline, wind direction trend, countdown.

    ProOnboard networkPolar

  • Session report and calibration findings

    After the session: is the wind vane misaligned, is the heel sensor right? With a correction value you can apply.

    ProOnboard network

Map, wind and navigation

Under way you are on the map. So that is where the tools are.

Depths and seamarks are available for European waters and part of the Caribbean. Go-To is a bearing, not a route around land.

Read more in the handbook

    High-contrast map style, night mode, own boat with course vector.

    FreeiPhone GPS

  • Offline maps

    Download your cruising areas, with no pack limit. An area at “Medium” brings harbours and channels at “Fine” by itself.

    Free

  • Wind now

    At the top of the map, in any situation: onboard network, manual wind, nearest station or model, with source, age and gusts.

    FreeOnboard networkInternet

  • Wind layer

    Model arrows and coastal stations on the map, switchable separately. Stations in seven colours, the model in colour from force 5.

    FreeInternet

  • Wind for sessions without a wind sensor

    After the session the app fetches hourly wind from the nearest station or the model and stores it with the session.

    FreeInternet

  • Go-To and routes

    Bearing and distance to a destination, waypoint sequences that advance automatically.

    FreeiPhone GPS

  • Waypoints with photos

    Up to ten photos per waypoint, to recognise the approach.

    FreeYour input

  • Harbour guide

    Your harbours with notes, toilet code, berthing fee and Wi-Fi. Harbours and anchorages from the map data are available offline.

    FreeYour input

  • GPX for the chartplotter

Analysis afterwards

Free tells you what happened. Pro tells you why, and what it cost.

Most sailors have no onboard network. That is why Pro works out whatever is possible from GPS alone and says how the value came about. If data is missing, the app gives the reason instead of an empty page.

Read more in the handbook

  • Race card

    Three sentences at the top of the analysis: start, extra distance, upwind performance.

    ProiPhone GPS

  • Manoeuvres one by one

    Speed dip, recovery time and cost in seconds per tack and gybe; best, median and worst.

    ProiPhone GPS

  • Manoeuvre loss in metres

    Against VMG before and after the manoeuvre.

    ProOnboard network

  • Manoeuvre learning curve

    Are your tacks getting cheaper over the season? With a separate series for training.

    ProiPhone GPS

  • Leg summary

    Progress to the mark, extra distance and manoeuvres per leg.

    ProiPhone GPS

  • Start summary

    Position on the line, speed build-up, choice of end and a history over the season. Speed against the polar target with wind from the onboard network.

    ProiPhone GPSOnboard network

  • Time-loss waterfall

    Where the time went, against your own best race; with wind from the onboard network against the polar.

    ProiPhone GPSOnboard network

  • Rounding quality and finish approach

    Dip and recovery at every mark, and the last 200 m before the finish.

    ProiPhone GPS

  • Your performance

    Level per topic across your scored races, with the trend since the last one.

    ProiPhone GPSYour input

  • Form trend and season comparison

    Average speed per wind band over the season and against last season. Polar percentage with wind from the onboard network.

    ProiPhone GPSOnboard network

    ProiPhone GPSInternet

  • Wind & shifts
